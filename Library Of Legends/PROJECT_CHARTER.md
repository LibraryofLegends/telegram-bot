VISION

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Library Of Legends aims to become a modern, scalable and modular
media management platform capable of organizing, indexing,
enriching and presenting digital media collections.

The platform shall support movies, television series,
music, books, comics, games, audiobooks,
magazines and future media types through
a unified architecture.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

MISSION

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Create the most maintainable, extensible and
well-documented personal media platform.

Every architectural decision shall prioritize:

• Simplicity
• Maintainability
• Scalability
• Performance
• Security
• Developer Experience

# 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the mandatory governance requirements for archiving controlled artifacts throughout the Library Of Legends Architecture Framework (LOAF).

The objective is to ensure long-term preservation, integrity, accessibility and traceability of archived information.

---

# 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- Documentation
- Policies
- Standards
- Workflows
- Source Code
- Releases
- Audit Records
- Review Records
- Approval Records
- Historical Repository Snapshots

---

# 3. Policy Statement

Archived information shall:

- remain complete
- remain authentic
- remain retrievable
- remain protected
- remain version-controlled
- remain auditable

Archived artifacts shall not be modified after archival unless explicitly authorized through governance procedures.

---

# 4. Archive Requirements

Every archived artifact shall include:

- Archive ID
- Classification
- Archive Date
- Version
- Responsible Owner
- Integrity Verification
- Retention Reference
- Archive Location

Archive metadata shall remain permanently associated with every archived artifact.

---

# 5. Archive Categories

The following archive categories are defined:

- Permanent Archive
- Historical Archive
- Governance Archive
- Technical Archive
- Release Archive
- Audit Archive
- Legal Archive
- Repository Snapshot Archive

Each category follows its assigned retention and access rules.

---

# 6. Archive Protection Requirements

Archive protection shall include:

- integrity verification
- access control
- backup protection
- redundancy where applicable
- audit logging
- periodic archive validation

Archived information shall be protected against unauthorized modification or deletion.

---

# 7. Roles and Responsibilities

Primary roles include:

- Repository Maintainer
- Documentation Owner
- Architecture Owner
- Security Officer
- Quality Manager
- Release Manager

Responsibilities are defined within the applicable RACI matrices.

---

# 8. Compliance Requirements

Compliance requires:

- approved archive workflow
- complete archive metadata
- documented archive verification
- repository registration
- retention compliance
- periodic archive audits

---

# 9. Exception Management

Archive exceptions require:

- documented justification
- governance approval
- risk assessment
- mitigation measures
- review schedule

Temporary exceptions shall include an expiration date.

---

# 10. Enforcement

Policy violations may result in:

- archive restoration
- governance escalation
- corrective actions
- audit findings
- compliance investigation

Unauthorized archive deletion is prohibited.

---

# 11. Monitoring

The following metrics shall be monitored:

- Archive Integrity Rate
- Archive Availability
- Retrieval Success Rate
- Metadata Completeness
- Archive Audit Findings
- Archive Validation Completion

Monitoring results shall be reviewed regularly.

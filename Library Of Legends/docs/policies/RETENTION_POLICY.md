# 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the mandatory governance requirements for retaining information assets throughout their required lifecycle within the Library Of Legends Architecture Framework (LOAF).

The objective is to ensure information remains available, traceable and protected for the required retention period.

---

# 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- Documentation
- Policies
- Standards
- Workflows
- Source Code
- Release Records
- Audit Records
- Approval Records
- Review Records
- Repository Metadata

---

# 3. Policy Statement

All retained information shall:

- follow approved retention periods
- remain protected
- remain traceable
- remain recoverable
- remain auditable
- follow classification requirements

Information shall not be removed before retention obligations have been satisfied.

---

# 4. Retention Requirements

Every retained artifact shall include:

- retention category
- retention owner
- retention period
- archive location
- review schedule
- disposition procedure

Retention requirements shall be documented before publication.

---

# 5. Retention Categories

The following categories are defined:

- Permanent
- Long-Term
- Operational
- Temporary
- Audit
- Legal
- Historical
- Repository Metadata

Each category follows its assigned retention schedule.

---

# 6. Retention Lifecycle

Every retained artifact follows:

Creation

↓

Classification

↓

Active Use

↓

Retention

↓

Review

↓

Archive

↓

Disposition Review

Historical artifacts may remain permanently archived.

---

# 7. Roles and Responsibilities

Primary roles include:

- Repository Maintainer
- Documentation Owner
- Architecture Owner
- Quality Manager
- Security Officer

Responsibilities are defined within the applicable RACI matrices.

---

# 8. Compliance Requirements

Compliance requires:

- approved retention schedules
- documented ownership
- repository traceability
- audit records
- archive registration
- periodic retention reviews

---

# 9. Exception Management

Retention exceptions require:

- documented justification
- governance approval
- legal review where applicable
- mitigation measures
- scheduled reassessment

Temporary exceptions shall include an expiration date.

---

# 10. Enforcement

Policy violations may result in:

- archive restoration
- governance escalation
- corrective actions
- audit findings
- compliance investigation

---

# 11. Monitoring

The following metrics shall be monitored:

- Retention Compliance Rate
- Archive Completeness
- Disposition Accuracy
- Recovery Success Rate
- Audit Findings
- Retention Review Completion

Monitoring results shall be reviewed regularly.

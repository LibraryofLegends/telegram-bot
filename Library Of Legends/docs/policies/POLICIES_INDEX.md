# 1. Purpose

The Policies Index is the official entry point for all governance policies defined within the Library Of Legends Architecture Framework (LOAF).

Policies establish mandatory organizational rules derived from the approved architectural principles.

Every policy published within the framework shall be listed in this document.

---

# 2. Scope

Policies govern mandatory behavior across all areas of the project, including:

- Documentation
- Development
- Security
- Releases
- Versioning
- Change Management
- Quality
- Operations
- Artificial Intelligence
- Repository Governance

---

# 3. Policy Philosophy

Policies transform architectural principles into enforceable project rules.

While principles define long-term direction, policies define mandatory behavior.

Policies are binding for every contributor participating in the project.

---

# 4. Policy Hierarchy

The governance hierarchy is defined as follows:

Principles

↓

Policies

↓

Standards

↓

Specifications

↓

Implementation

Every lower layer shall comply with the policies defined above it.

# 6. Governance

Every policy shall:

- be documented
- be version controlled
- be reviewed
- be approved
- remain traceable
- define measurable compliance requirements

---

# 7. Relationship to Principles

Every policy shall directly support one or more approved principles.

Policies shall never contradict governing principles.

Conflicts require Architecture Decision Record (ADR) approval.

---

# 8. Relationship to Standards

Standards define technical implementation requirements derived from policies.

Policies define *what* shall be enforced.

Standards define *how* enforcement is achieved.

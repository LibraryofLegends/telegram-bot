# 1. Purpose

This document defines the quality principles governing every deliverable produced within the Library Of Legends Architecture Framework (LOAF).

Quality shall be embedded into every activity rather than verified only after implementation.

---

# 2. Quality Philosophy

Quality is achieved through disciplined planning, consistent execution, continuous verification and ongoing improvement.

Every artifact produced by the project shall meet defined quality expectations before it is considered complete.

Quality is the responsibility of every contributor.

---

# 3. Core Quality Principles

## 3.1 Quality by Design

Quality shall be planned from the beginning rather than added later.

---

## 3.2 Prevention over Correction

Preventing defects is preferable to correcting defects after implementation.

---

## 3.3 Consistency

Processes, documentation, architecture and implementation shall follow established standards.

---

## 3.4 Completeness

Every deliverable shall satisfy all documented requirements before approval.

---

## 3.5 Traceability

Every implementation shall be traceable to approved documentation and recorded changes.

---

## 3.6 Verification

Every important deliverable shall be verified before release.

---

## 3.7 Validation

The delivered solution shall satisfy its intended purpose and business objectives.

---

## 3.8 Continuous Improvement

Lessons learned shall be incorporated into future work.

Processes shall evolve continuously.

---

## 3.9 Transparency

Quality activities shall be documented and auditable.

---

## 3.10 Accountability

Quality responsibilities shall be clearly assigned and maintained.

---

# 4. Quality Objectives

The framework shall strive to achieve:

- Reliability
- Maintainability
- Scalability
- Stability
- Security
- Accuracy
- Consistency
- Performance
- Availability
- Documentation Quality

Quality objectives shall support long-term sustainability.

---

# 5. Quality Assurance

Quality Assurance shall include:

- Standards Compliance
- Architecture Reviews
- Documentation Reviews
- Process Audits
- Risk Assessments
- Quality Planning

QA activities focus on preventing defects before implementation.

---

# 6. Quality Control

Quality Control activities include:

- Code Reviews
- Functional Testing
- Integration Testing
- Regression Testing
- Documentation Validation
- Release Verification

QC activities confirm that deliverables satisfy defined requirements.

---

# 7. Continuous Improvement

Improvement activities shall include:

- Review findings
- Lessons learned
- Process optimization
- Automation opportunities
- Root cause analysis
- Preventive actions

Quality improvement shall be continuous.

---

# 8. Metrics and Measurement

Quality shall be evaluated using measurable indicators where appropriate.

Examples include:

- Documentation completeness
- Test coverage
- Review completion
- Defect density
- Build success rate
- Change success rate
- Release quality
- Architecture compliance

Metrics shall support improvement rather than punishment.

---

# 9. Governance

Quality governance shall ensure:

- consistent reviews
- objective evaluations
- documented approvals
- continuous monitoring
- compliance with project standards

Exceptions require documented approval.

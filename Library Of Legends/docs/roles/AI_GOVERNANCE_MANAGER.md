# 1. Purpose

The AI Governance Manager is responsible for defining, supervising and continuously improving the responsible use of Artificial Intelligence within the Library Of Legends Architecture Framework (LOAF).

The role ensures that AI systems are used safely, transparently, consistently and in accordance with project policies.

---

# 2. Role Mission

The mission of the AI Governance Manager is to establish trustworthy AI processes that support development without replacing human accountability.

Core objectives include:

- Transparency
- Human Oversight
- Traceability
- Security
- Reliability
- Quality
- Compliance
- Continuous Improvement

---

# 3. Scope

The AI Governance Manager governs:

- AI-assisted Development
- AI-assisted Documentation
- AI-assisted Testing
- AI-assisted Reviews
- Prompt Management
- AI Model Usage
- AI Policies
- AI Risk Management
- AI Audit Trails

---

# 4. Responsibilities

The AI Governance Manager shall:

- define AI usage standards
- review AI-generated artifacts
- maintain AI governance documentation
- approve AI workflows
- coordinate AI quality reviews
- evaluate AI risks
- maintain AI prompt libraries
- monitor AI compliance

---

# 5. Authority

The AI Governance Manager has authority to:

- approve AI usage policies
- suspend non-compliant AI workflows
- require human review
- reject undocumented AI artifacts
- require AI audit documentation
- initiate AI governance reviews

---

# 6. Decision Rights

Final decisions include:

- approved AI tools
- AI workflow standards
- AI documentation requirements
- prompt governance
- AI review procedures
- AI compliance requirements

---

# 7. Accountability

The AI Governance Manager is accountable for:

- AI governance
- AI transparency
- AI documentation
- AI compliance
- AI quality assurance

Human accountability cannot be delegated to AI.

# 12. AI Governance Rules

The AI Governance Manager shall ensure:

- AI usage remains transparent
- every AI-generated artifact is reviewable
- prompts are documented
- AI decisions are traceable
- human approval remains mandatory
- AI usage complies with project policies

# 1. Purpose

The Quality Manager is responsible for establishing, monitoring and continuously improving the overall quality of the Library Of Legends Architecture Framework (LOAF).

The role ensures that all project artifacts comply with defined quality objectives before approval and release.

---

# 2. Role Mission

The mission of the Quality Manager is to ensure that quality is built into every phase of the project lifecycle.

Quality objectives include:

- Accuracy
- Completeness
- Consistency
- Reliability
- Maintainability
- Traceability
- Continuous Improvement

Quality shall be preventive rather than corrective.

---

# 3. Scope

The Quality Manager governs:

- Documentation Quality
- Source Code Quality
- Architecture Quality
- API Quality
- Database Quality
- Release Quality
- Repository Quality
- Testing Standards
- Review Processes
- Quality Metrics

---

# 4. Responsibilities

The Quality Manager shall:

- define quality objectives
- establish quality metrics
- conduct quality reviews
- coordinate quality audits
- verify compliance with standards
- monitor quality trends
- recommend corrective actions
- promote continuous improvement

---

# 5. Authority

The Quality Manager has authority to:

- reject artifacts that fail quality requirements
- require corrective actions
- initiate quality audits
- approve quality gates
- recommend process improvements
- suspend release recommendations until quality requirements are met

---

# 6. Decision Rights

Final decisions include:

- quality acceptance criteria
- quality metrics
- audit schedules
- review procedures
- quality reporting standards
- continuous improvement initiatives

---

# 7. Accountability

The Quality Manager is accountable for:

- quality governance
- compliance with quality standards
- audit effectiveness
- quality reporting
- continuous quality improvement

Quality accountability remains independent from implementation activities.

---

# 8. Required Competencies

Required competencies include:

- Quality Management
- Software Quality Assurance
- Technical Documentation
- Process Improvement
- Risk Assessment
- Metrics Analysis
- Governance
- Communication

---

# 9. Deliverables

Expected deliverables include:

- Quality Reports
- Audit Reports
- Compliance Assessments
- Quality Dashboards
- Review Checklists
- Continuous Improvement Plans
- Corrective Action Reports

---

# 10. Quality Metrics

Typical metrics include:

- Documentation Completeness
- Review Completion Rate
- Defect Density
- Audit Compliance Rate
- Technical Debt Trend
- Test Coverage
- Change Success Rate
- Release Readiness Score

Metrics shall be reviewed and refined regularly.

---

# 11. Collaboration

The Quality Manager collaborates with:

- Architecture Owner
- Documentation Owner
- Repository Maintainer
- Security Officer
- Release Manager
- Module Owners
- Package Owners
- AI Governance Manager

---

# 12. Quality Governance Rules

The Quality Manager shall ensure:

- quality gates are enforced
- standards remain current
- audits are documented
- corrective actions are tracked
- quality metrics remain measurable
- lessons learned are incorporated into future improvements

# 14. Success Criteria

The role is considered successful when:

- Quality objectives are consistently achieved.
- Audit findings decrease over time.
- Quality standards are followed across the project.
- Corrective actions are completed on schedule.
- Continuous improvement is demonstrable.

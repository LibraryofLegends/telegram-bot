# 1. Purpose

This document defines responsibility assignments for identifying, assessing, mitigating, monitoring and reviewing risks throughout the Library Of Legends Architecture Framework (LOAF).

Its purpose is to establish a proactive and measurable risk management process.

---

# 2. Scope

The matrix applies to:

- Risk Identification
- Risk Analysis
- Risk Assessment
- Risk Prioritization
- Risk Treatment
- Risk Monitoring
- Risk Reporting
- Risk Review
- Residual Risk Acceptance
- Continuous Risk Improvement

# 3. RACI Definitions

| Code | Definition |
|------|------------|
| R | Responsible |
| A | Accountable |
| C | Consulted |
| I | Informed |

Rules

- Exactly one Accountable role.
- Every identified risk shall have an assigned owner.
- Risk assessments shall be documented.
- Risk reviews shall be performed periodically.

# 5. Risk Management Principles

Risk management shall:

- be proactive
- be evidence-based
- be continuously monitored
- support informed decisions
- prioritize critical risks
- encourage continuous improvement

---

# 6. Escalation Rules

Risk escalation follows:

Risk Owner

↓

Security Officer

↓

Architecture Owner

↓

Governance Board

Critical risks require immediate governance review.

---

# 7. Risk Governance Guidelines

Risk management shall:

- maintain a centralized risk register
- assign ownership for every risk
- review risks regularly
- document mitigation actions
- monitor residual risks
- maintain complete audit trails
